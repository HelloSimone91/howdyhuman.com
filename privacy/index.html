Privacy Policy

Values Dictionary for iOS · Last updated September 17, 2026

Values Dictionary is built to work without an account or a backend. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information or your saved values.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Values Dictionary iOS app by Howdy Human. It does not cover the Howdy Human website or any third-party service you may choose to use from your device.

Information the app does not collect

Values Dictionary does not collect or transmit personal information, saved values, usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or messages. The app has no accounts, advertising, in-app analytics, tracking tools, or third-party data-sharing services.

Saved values stay on your device

When you save a value, the app stores that selection locally on your device so it can appear in your Saved list. Howdy Human cannot access those saved values. Because there is no account or cloud backup provided by the app, your saved values may be removed if you delete the app or erase its data.

Sharing is your choice

If you choose to use a sharing feature, iOS sends the content you select to the app or service you choose through Apple’s system Share Sheet. Howdy Human does not receive a copy of what you share. The recipient service’s own privacy policy applies to anything you send there.

Children’s privacy

Because Values Dictionary does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, we will update this page and revise the date above.
